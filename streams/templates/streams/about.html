FAQ

Where does the streaming data come from?

+

Streaming data is collected for albums every day using a proprietary collection and estimation model. The streaming data provided only captures streams recorded on Spotify.

How does this work?

+

When an album is released it is added to our collection of albums. We proceed to collect streaming data on each song in the album for the duration of a year. If an album has songs that were released before the full album, the streams prior to release are removed. This enables us to accurately track the growth of albums upon release without having singles create biased data.

How is percentile calculated?

+

The total days since release for an album is calculated initially. Once found, a dataset is created with all of our other records that have the same days since release. Once the dataset is found, it is fitted to the most optimal distribution and percentile is calculated through a cumulative density function.

How is revenue calculated?

+

There is no set amount that Spotify pays per stream. The best estimate that can be found on the internet comes from an independent label with a 350+ album catalog that generates around 1.5b annual streams. According to their most recent report, the average payout per stream from Spotify was $0.00348 which is what we use to estimate revenue.

How is growth calculated?

+

Growth is calculated by dividing the total streams on day (n) by the total streams on day one.
